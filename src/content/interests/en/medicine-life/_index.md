  - title: Reading a lab report
    body: Don't panic over a value slightly above or below range. Reference intervals are statistics, not verdicts.
  - title: Home medicine kit
    body: Antipyretic, bandages, iodine, thermometer. Clearing out expired items regularly beats stockpiling.
  - title: Do colds need medicine?
    body: Most colds are viral. Medicine mostly makes you more comfortable, not "faster to recover".
  - title: Health checkup report
    body: Read the summary page first, then go back to the abnormal items. Don't frighten yourself, and don't dismiss it either.
  - title: The cost of staying up late
    body: The body keeps a ledger. What you borrow today, it collects at a moment you least expect.
  - title: About drinking water
    body: Eight glasses a day is a rough rule. Watching the color is far more reliable than counting cups.
  - title: Sitting and your neck
    body: Two minutes of walking every hour beats any massager. Simple in principle, hard in practice.
